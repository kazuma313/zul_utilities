Research Project

Consumer Behavior Research

Understanding customers, shaping better strategies

01

Research Overview

This study examines how urban consumers aged 18 to 45 choose, compare and buy everyday products. It combines a 350-respondent survey with four focus groups held in two cities during March and April 2026.

02

Objectives

  • Identify the factors that drive purchase decisions
  • Measure price sensitivity across income groups
  • Map the role of brand trust and online channels
03

Target Audience

Age range18 - 45 years old
GenderAll
LocationUrban areas, 2 cities
Income levelMiddle to upper
Sample size350 respondents
350 of 500 invited took part
04

Research Methods

  • Online survey

    350 respondents, 24 questions, March 2026

  • Focus group discussion

    4 groups of 8 participants in Medan and Jakarta

  • Data analysis

    Descriptive statistics and thematic coding

05

Key Findings

Product quality is the top priority72%of respondents rank quality as the most important factor
Price sensitivity matters68%compare prices before making a purchase
Brand trust influences purchase65%choose brands they already trust
Online shopping is growing60%prefer to shop online for convenience and promotions
06

Customer Insights

  • 53.7%Quality & price
  • 24.4%Promotions
  • 14.6%Convenience
  • 4.9%Brand
  • 2.4%Others

More than half of the respondents name quality and price together as their main reason to buy. Promotions matter for a quarter, mostly among younger buyers.

“Consumers reward quality first and price second; promotions win only when both are equal.”
07

Market Trends

  • Rise of e-commerce

    Online purchases grew 18% year on year

  • Mobile influence

    7 in 10 respondents research products on their phone first

  • Sustainability matters

    38% pay more for products with less packaging

08

Recommendations

  • Focus on quality

    Keep quality visible on packaging and product pages

  • Competitive pricing

    Match the two largest competitors on core items

  • Strengthen brand trust

    Publish reviews and guarantees where customers compare
