Maximum items: paragraphs 2, cards 3 (three_cards) or 2 (two_cards), overview_items 4, team people 3, people 4, timeline columns 4 with 2 items each, chart series 3 with 2 to 8 values, images 3, testimonials 4.

Rules:
1. Keep the order above. A "section" slide may be repeated to open another part; place it directly before the slide it introduces.
2. Titles: at most 4 words. cover title: at most 30 characters.
3. "text", paragraphs: under 450 characters. Card body: under 200. Timeline item: under 80. Testimonial text: under 170.
4. chart "values" are numbers. "categories" and every "values" list have the same length. type: column, bar, line, pie, doughnut, radar, area.
5. Use ONLY facts, numbers, names and image files given by the user. No material for a slide: leave the slide out. Never invent people, quotes or statistics.
6. "image" fields are local file names the user gave. Leave them out otherwise.
7. Plain text only: no **bold**, no bullet characters, no emojis, no line breaks inside a string.
8. Write all text in the language the user asks for. Keep the field names in English.
